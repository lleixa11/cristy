1. Acceptance of terms

If you do not agree with any part of these terms, please do not book or use our services.

The short version We show up on time, groom your pet with care, and ask that you give us 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel. We need to know about health issues and behavioral concerns in advance so we can keep your pet — and our groomer — safe.

2. Our services

Cristy Mobile Pet Grooming provides mobile pet grooming services in South Florida. Our van comes to your home, and all grooming is performed inside the van by a trained groomer.

Our current services include:

  • Full groom — bath, haircut, styling, ear cleaning and nail trim.
  • Bath + brush — shampoo, full dry and brush-out.
  • Nail trim — quick and gentle nail care.
  • Teeth cleaning — gentle brushing for fresh breath.
  • Cat grooming — calm, gentle grooming for cats.

We reserve the right to add, modify or discontinue services at any time.

3. Booking & scheduling

Appointments can be booked through our website booking form, by phone, or through our online booking system. All appointments are subject to availability.

When you book, we will confirm a time window for arrival. Our groomer will notify you if there are any delays (traffic, previous appointments running long, weather). We appreciate your patience.

Please have your pet ready at the scheduled time. If you are not available when we arrive, we will wait a maximum of 15 minutes. After that, the appointment may be treated as a no-show.

If your pet has special handling needs or if you have specific grooming requests, please tell us at the time of booking so we can prepare accordingly.

4. Cancellations & no-shows

We understand that life happens. To keep our schedule fair for all clients, we ask for the following:

  • 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment at no charge.
  • Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged a fee of $30 per scheduled appointment.
  • No-shows (you are not home or your pet is not available when we arrive) will be charged the full price of the scheduled service.

Repeated cancellations or no-shows may result in the requirement of prepayment or a deposit for future appointments.

If we need to cancel or reschedule (due to severe weather, mechanical issues, or emergency), you will be notified as soon as possible and we will work with you to find a new time at no penalty.

5. Payment & pricing

Payment is due at the time of service, before your pet is returned to you. We accept cash, major credit/debit cards, and digital payments.

Prices are based on your pet's size, breed, coat condition, temperament, and the services requested. We will provide an estimate when you book. Final pricing may vary if we discover matting, excessive shedding, fleas/ticks, or other conditions that require additional time and care. Any price changes will be communicated to you before the service is completed.

Additional fees may apply for:

  • Severe matting (de-matting or shave-down)
  • Flea or tick treatment
  • Aggressive behavior requiring special handling or extra time
  • Services that take significantly longer than anticipated

If payment is not received at the time of service, we reserve the right to charge a late fee and to require prepayment for future appointments.

6. Pet health & vaccinations

Your pet's safety is our first priority. By booking, you confirm that:

  • Your pet is in good general health and free from contagious illnesses.
  • Your pet is current on rabies vaccination as required by Florida law (F.S. 828.30, which requires rabies vaccination for all dogs, cats and ferrets 4 months of age or older). Proof of vaccination may be requested.
  • You have disclosed all known medical conditions, allergies, injuries, recent surgeries, medications, skin conditions, and behavioral concerns before the appointment.

If your pet shows signs of illness (vomiting, diarrhea, coughing, lethargy, etc.) on the day of the appointment, please contact us to reschedule. We cannot groom sick pets.

If we discover fleas, ticks, or other parasites during grooming, we reserve the right to treat your pet with appropriate products and add the corresponding fee to your total.

We do not groom pets with certain medical conditions that make grooming unsafe. If you are unsure whether your pet qualifies, contact us before booking.

7. Matted coats

Matted coats are uncomfortable for pets and can hide skin problems, infections, and injuries. De-matting is stressful and carries risk to your pet.

If your pet's coat is severely matted, we may recommend a shave-down for your pet's welfare rather than attempting to de-mat. You will be informed before we proceed.

You understand and accept that:

  • Shaving a matted coat may reveal pre-existing skin conditions.
  • Nicks, cuts, or irritation can occur when removing tight mats.
  • Additional fees apply for de-matting or shave-downs.

8. Behavior & safety

You must disclose any history of biting, aggression, or difficult behavior before the appointment.

Our groomer may use humane restraint or a muzzle if necessary for the safety of your pet or the groomer. If your pet becomes excessively stressed, aggressive, or poses a safety risk, we reserve the right to stop the groom. Charges may still apply for the time spent and travel.

For safety reasons, we ask that clients remain outside the van during grooming unless the groomer requests assistance. Please keep children and other pets away from the work area.

We reserve the right to refuse or discontinue service if we believe the environment is unsafe, if the client is disrespectful or aggressive, or if the pet cannot be groomed safely.

9. Limitation of liability

Grooming involves inherent risks, including nicks, cuts, abrasions, skin irritation, nail bleeding, and stress-related reactions. While we take every precaution, these risks cannot be eliminated entirely.

We are not responsible for:

  • Pre-existing conditions (skin issues, lumps, ear infections, injuries, etc.) that are discovered or aggravated during grooming.
  • Reactions to grooming products if you have not disclosed known allergies.
  • Stress-related issues or health problems that arise from your pet's pre-existing medical conditions.
  • Any injury or damage caused by your pet's behavior that you failed to disclose.
  • Damage to your property caused by your pet before, during, or after the appointment.

In the event of an emergency, we will attempt to contact you. If we cannot reach you, we are authorized to seek veterinary care at your expense.
