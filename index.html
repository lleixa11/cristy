Meet Dayana.

Behind Cristy Mobile Pet Grooming is Dayana, the person who puts care, patience and attention into every appointment. For her, grooming is about more than a fresh coat — it's about making pets feel comfortable, cared for and their absolute best.

She's built the experience around the little details that make a difference, from the way each pet is handled to the finishing touches that send them home looking and feeling their best.

"Every pet deserves to be treated with patience, care and a little extra love."
